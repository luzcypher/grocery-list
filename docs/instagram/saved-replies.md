# Instagram saved replies

Instagram has its own quick replies for DMs. Set them up once, then answer a DM in two taps.

**How to add one:** in the Instagram app go to **Settings → Business tools and controls → Saved replies → +**.
(If you don't see it, open any DM, tap the **⋯** or the **+** next to the message box and look for **Saved replies**.)
For each one, type the **shortcut** and paste the **message**. To use it: in a DM, type the shortcut and tap the reply that pops up.

---

## Shortcut: `lista` (Spanish, someone DMs LISTA)

¡Gracias por seguirme! 🛒 Aquí está tu app gratis: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/

Para ponerla en tu teléfono:
• Android: abre el link en Chrome → toca los tres puntitos ⋮ → "Instalar aplicación".
• iPhone: ábrelo en Safari → toca Compartir (cuadrito con flecha) → "Agregar a inicio".

Adentro toca "Ayuda": ahí viene todo paso a paso. ¿Tienes un negocio? Cuéntame, también hago apps para negocios 😉

## Shortcut: `list` (English, someone DMs LIST)

Thanks for the follow! 🛒 Here's your free app: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/

To put it on your phone:
• Android: open the link in Chrome → tap the three dots ⋮ → "Install app".
• iPhone: open it in Safari → tap Share (square with an arrow) → "Add to Home Screen".

Inside, tap "Help": it walks you through everything. Have a business? Tell me about it, I build apps for businesses too 😉
