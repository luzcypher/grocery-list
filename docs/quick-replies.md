# Quick replies for new leads

Save these in **WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools → Quick replies**.
Type the shortcut (for example `/plan`) in any chat and the message fills in.
On an iPhone you can also save the English ones as **Text Replacements**
(Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement) to use in regular text messages.

Planner link: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/apps/plan/
App ideas page: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/apps/

## /plan — first reply to someone interested

**Español**
> ¡Hola! Gracias por escribir 🙌 Para armar la idea de tu app, contesta estas preguntas rápidas (2 minutos) y te llega un mini plan: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/apps/plan/
> Cuando lo tengas, mándamelo desde ahí y lo platicamos.

**English**
> Hi! Thanks for reaching out 🙌 To shape your app idea, answer these quick questions (2 minutes) and you'll get a mini plan: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/apps/plan/
> When you're done, send it to me from that page and we'll talk.

## /ideas — someone who isn't sure what they need

**Español**
> Aquí tienes ejemplos de apps que hago para negocios: pedidos, citas, listas para recoger y tarjetas de lealtad: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/apps/
> ¿Cuál se parece más a lo que necesitas?

**English**
> Here are examples of apps I build for businesses: orders, appointments, pickup lists and loyalty cards: https://luzcypher.github.io/grocery-list/apps/
> Which one is closest to what you need?

## /gracias — after they send their plan

**Español**
> ¡Recibí tu plan, gracias! Lo reviso y te escribo hoy con ideas y los siguientes pasos. ¿Qué horario te queda mejor para platicar 15 minutos?

**English**
> Got your plan, thanks! I'll look it over and get back to you today with ideas and next steps. What time works best for a 15-minute chat?

## /seguimiento — no answer after a couple of days

**Español**
> ¡Hola! Solo para saber si pudiste ver el plan de tu app. Si tienes dudas, aquí estoy. 🙂

**English**
> Hi! Just checking whether you had a chance to look at your app plan. Happy to answer any questions. 🙂
